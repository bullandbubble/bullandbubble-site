Privacy

Last updated 4 October 2026.

Short version: this site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and it never asks you for personal information.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. The fonts, images and video are all served from this site, not from third parties. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes (GitHub's documentation, GitHub's privacy statement).

Links to other sites

Links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Threads and Facebook take you to those platforms, and their own privacy policies apply once you are there.

If you email us

Mail sent to hello@bullandbubble.com is forwarded by ImprovMX to our inbox, which is hosted by Google (Gmail). We use your message only to reply to you, keep it only as long as we need to, and never sell or share it. Ask us at any time and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you. If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
